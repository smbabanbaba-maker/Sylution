# Jagorar sabon tsari — SYLUTION LTD

## Jigon gani
**Premium editorial engineering**: shafi mai tsafta da kyan gani na musamman, ba cunkoso ba; ja mai duhu, fari mai dumi da baki kamar tsarin SYLUTION da aka riga aka amince da shi.

## Ka'idojin tsari
- Mai shiga ya fahimci da sauri cewa SYLUTION tana gina na'urori da manhajoji, tana ƙera ayyukan fasaha, kuma tana koyar da horo na aiki da hannu.
- Hero ya zama rubutu mai ƙarfi da tsari na musamman, ba drone ko hoton gona ba. Hotunan sauran ayyuka za su kasance ne kawai idan suna tabbatar da wani aiki ko samfurin da ake bayani a kai.
- A matakin farko a nuna hanyoyi uku dabam: ayyukan injiniyanci, kayayyaki/ayyukan ci gaba (Sysmart Agro a bayyane a matsayin aikin ci gaba, ba kaya a shago ba), da horon SYLUTION Academy (a tambayi samuwa da jadawali).
- A cire maimaitattun katuna, bayanai marasa ƙarin amfani, marquee da sauran sassa masu tsawaita shafi ba tare da ƙarin bayani ba.
- Mai sauya harshe ya kasance a bayyane ko da a waya: maɓallin yanzu da tutar yare/ƙasa, sannan jerin sunaye masu cikakken bayani. Turanci—Birtaniya; Hausa—Najeriya; Faransanci—Faransa. Larabci ya samu alamar rubutun Larabci mai tsaka-tsaki, domin Larabci harshe ne da ƙasashe da dama ke magana da shi, ba shi da tuta guda ɗaya da ta dace.
- A kiyaye sauƙin amfani, rubutu mai karantawa, motsi mai sauƙi, gumaka masu ma'ana, da daidaiton waya/ƙaramin allo.

## Launuka
A kiyaye ja, fari da baki na SYLUTION; a yi amfani da ja a matsayin lafazi mai iyaka, ba bango mai duhu a ko'ina ba. Fari mai dumi ya kasance babban bango, baki/ink ya ɗauki rubutu, sannan jan brand ya jaddada kalmomi ko aikin da ake nema.

## Tsarin shafi
- Hero: taken da ke bayyana gini da koyarwa, taƙaitaccen misalan fasaha, da hanyoyin sabis / aikin Sysmart Agro / horo.
- Gaba: ƙwarewar da SYLUTION ke bayarwa, aikin Sysmart Agro tare da matsayin ci gaba, bayani kaɗan kan horo, sannan shaidar aiki mai amfani.
- A cire sassan da ke faɗin bayanin Sysmart ko ƙwarewar fasaha iri ɗaya sau da yawa.

## Mu'amala da motsi
- Hanyoyi masu sauƙin fahimta, babban matakin da ake zaɓa a sarari, focus mai gani, da `aria-label` / `aria-expanded` da ya dace.
- A tabbatar da selector ba ya ɓoye a waya, kuma kada tutoci su dogara da emoji ko font na na'urar mai amfani.
- A mutunta `prefers-reduced-motion`; a guji motsi na ado da ba ya taimaka wa fahimta.

## Rubutu da murya
A faɗi gaskiya, a yi amfani da kalmomi kai tsaye, a guji taken tallace-tallace marasa takamaiman ma'ana. A nuna bambanci a fili tsakanin sabis, aikin samfurin da ke ci gaba, da horo da samuwarsa sai an tabbatar.

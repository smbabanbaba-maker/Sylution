Who We Are

SYLUTION LTD

Innovation for Sustainable Agriculture

SYLUTION LTD is a technology, innovation, and solutions company that leverages Artificial Intelligence (AI), Internet of Things (IoT), Renewable Energy, Smart Devices, and Climate-Smart Technologies to solve problems in agriculture, environment, security, and community development.

Our vision is to become one of Africa's leading AgriTech and Climate Technology companies by delivering affordable, sustainable, and effective farming solutions.

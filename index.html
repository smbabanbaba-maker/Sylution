Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers

What is smart irrigation and how does it work?

Smart irrigation uses IoT sensors and AI to monitor soil moisture, weather conditions, and crop needs. The system automatically delivers the right amount of water at the right time, reducing water waste by up to 60% while improving crop yields.

Do you provide installation and training?

Yes! We provide complete installation services and comprehensive training for farmers. Our team ensures you understand how to use all systems effectively, and we offer ongoing support to answer any questions.

How much can I save with your solutions?

Our clients typically save 40-60% on water usage, 30% on energy costs (with solar integration), and increase crop yields by 25-50%. The return on investment is usually achieved within 1-2 growing seasons.

Do you serve small-scale farmers?

Absolutely! We have solutions for farms of all sizes. Our Starter package is specifically designed for small-scale farmers, making smart agriculture accessible and affordable for everyone.

What areas do you cover in Nigeria?

We currently operate across Nigeria with our headquarters in Kano. We serve farmers in all 36 states and are expanding to other African countries. Contact us to check availability in your area.
